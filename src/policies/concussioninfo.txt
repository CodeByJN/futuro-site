Concussion Information and Protocol to recovery 
from Sanjeev Parmar 
Parents, 
​ 
I wanted to share some information on concussions with you, as I cut my professional playing career short due to having to retire because of a serious concussion. I went through a long period of time where I would get headaches for a number of reasons, especially when I was hungry. Even to this day, my head will start to hurt due to being hungry. 
It's a good idea to have this information readily available and be well versed on this subject.
