Every Day
In Every Way.

Our mission is to produce professional, international and university players, and to give every player the chance to reach their full potential.

We play Joga Bonito: possession-based, attacking soccer that gives players the freedom and confidence to show their creativity. Off the ball, every player works to win it back fast. Off the field, we hold players to the same standard in school, because education comes first.

Inside the program

Three sessions and a match, every week.

This is not recreational soccer. Expect a pro-academy week, honest feedback and a high bar on and off the field.

What your player can expect
  • 3 sessions + a matchevery week, all year
  • Strength & speedweekly session grouped by biological age (13+), plus sprint mechanics
  • Goalkeepersan extra session every week with a GK coach
  • Summer camp4 weeks with pro-academy coaches from across Europe
  • School comes firstminimum grades required, with an education check-in every term
