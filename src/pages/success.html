Futuro Legends

The best of the best.

They all started at Futuro. Here is where they are now.

Noah Abatneh

NOW: CANADA MEN'S NATIONAL TEAM · CPL CHAMPION
  • Futuro's first senior Canada men's national team call-up
  • Won the 2025 Canadian Premier League title with Atlético Ottawa
  • Canada U20 national team
  • Signed with S.S. Lazio U17 in Italy's Serie A; later played for York United
  • As a Futuro player: offered a contract by AS Roma (2016), on trial with Arsenal, and offered a contract by Fulham FC

Sadie Sider-Echenberg

NOW: PRO, KRISTIANSTADS DFF (SWEDEN)
  • Canada futsal national team
  • Canada U20 camp
  • Played with Le Havre AC; trials with PSV Eindhoven, Hoffenheim and Racing Louisville
  • OUA First Team All-Star at the University of Ottawa

Antoine Coupland

NOW: PRO (WHITECAPS · ATLÉTICO OTTAWA)
  • Short-term agreement with Vancouver Whitecaps FC (MLS)
  • Signed with Whitecaps FC 2 in MLS NEXT Pro (2023)
  • Signed with HNK Rijeka in Croatia; named to Canada U20 list
  • Scored 5 goals against Cardiff City on Futuro's 2016 UK tour

Maya Hindson

NOW: UNIVERSITY OF SOUTH FLORIDA (NCAA D1)
  • Signed with Arsenal FC
  • Selected for the Canada U20 national team
  • Ranked #1 in FTF Canada's 2024 graduating-class rankings (girls)
  • Trained with OGC Nice

Elage Bah

NOW: PRO, CANADIAN PREMIER LEAGUE
  • Vancouver FC in the CPL: 14 appearances and 2 goals in his first season
  • Signed with the Vancouver Whitecaps U17 academy; played for Whitecaps FC 2
  • Named to Canada U20 list
  • Scored twice in Futuro's 6–0 win over Leicester City (2016)

Tyr Duhaney-Walker

NOW: PRO, ATLÉTICO OTTAWA
  • 2025 Canadian Premier League champion alongside fellow Futuro graduate Noah Abatneh
  • Signed with Atlético Ottawa (2023) and re-signed in 2025
  • Scored against Bristol Rovers on Futuro's 2016 UK tour

Eric Lajeunesse

NOW: PACIFIC FC (CPL) · UBC
  • U SPORTS Rookie of the Year at UBC
  • Signed a U SPORTS contract with Pacific FC in the CPL
  • Signed with the Vancouver Whitecaps MLS reserve team
  • Joined Futuro on October 1, 2011, the academy's first day

Molly Hale

NOW: COMMITTED TO UCLA
  • Canada U17 national team
  • Committed to UCLA, one of the top NCAA Division I programs
  • Trials with Le Havre AC and OGC Nice in France

Michael Peres

THE NEXT WAVE · CLASS OF 2026
  • Signed with CF Montréal
  • Ranked #1 CDM in FTF Canada's 2026 graduating-class rankings
  • Vancouver Whitecaps trial
