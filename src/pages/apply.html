Players join all year round.

There's no single deadline. Players can be placed in the program at any time of year, after an identification session with our staff.

How to join.

  1. Register for a tryout

    Tell us about your player with the tryout form. It takes about two minutes.

  2. Identification session

    We invite your player to train with the group so our coaches can see their level and fit.

  3. Placement & registration

    If it's a match, we place your player on a team and send the registration and fee details.

Financial assistance.

These organizations help families with registration fees. You can apply to more than one.

Jumpstart

Funding for kids aged 4–18 from families in financial need, for registration, equipment or transportation.

jumpstart.canadiantire.ca

KidSport

Grants for children 18 and under to remove the financial barriers to organized sport.

kidsportcanada.ca

Their Opportunity

Helps families enroll kids in local sport. Funded players "pay it forward" through community service.

theiropportunity.ca

Applying with funding

  1. Register your player in our online registration system, and stop at the payment screen.
  2. Email {{email}} with your funding application number, the amount requested, and your plan for any balance (instalments are an option).
  3. Your player is placed on a roster once proof of the funding application and any balance payment are received.
