Highlights

The Futuro Standard

High intensity.
Elite skill. Every session.

Futuro is not recreational soccer. Players train at the intensity of a pro academy and are held to clear standards on the field, in the classroom and in how they carry themselves.

01

Technical genius

Technical speed: how quick and clean a player is on the ball. Receiving, running with the ball, retaining it and releasing it, trained until it holds up under pressure.

02

Pro-level intensity

A dedicated "Chaos Coach" runs high-intensity, game-realistic sessions that build fast transitions and quick decisions under pressure.

03

Tactical intelligence

A possession-based, attacking style. Players learn when and how to attack and defend, whether their team is in balance or out of balance.

04

Physically capable

Speed, endurance, agility and core strength, coached by specialists including the Bahrain national team's sprint coach.

05

Mentally strong

Players stay confident and positive, control their emotions, and move on quickly from mistakes.

06

Educationally sound

Players must meet minimum grade standards to stay in the academy, and each one meets our Education Advisor every reporting period.

What parents
should expect

Demanding training, honest feedback and a high bar every week. In return, your child gets coaching from people who have worked inside pro clubs and national-team programs, and a proven route to university and professional soccer. Our motto, Every Day In Every Way, means we expect that effort from every player, on and off the field.

Programs

A clear pathway, age 7 to 18.

Competitive academy teams for players aged 7–18 from Eastern Ontario and Western Quebec, plus exhibition matches against top opposition.

U7–U10

Learning to Train

1v1 attacking, ball mastery, agility and confidence. Small-sided 5v5–7v7.

U11–U12

Learning to Train

9v9. Receiving and distributing, overloads, defending and decision-making.

U13–U14

Train to Train

11v11. Pro-club academy and ID-camp exposure; position-specific work.

U15–U16

Train to Train

Youth national-team pathways, university showcases and individual fitness plans.

U17–U18

Train to Compete

University recruitment, semi-pro and pro trials, and national-team exposure.
