Every Day In Every Way.

Our motto is a daily reminder of what we expect: excellence in all seven pillars, on and off the field.

Mission

To produce professional, international and university-level players, and to give every player the chance to reach their full potential.

Vision

Futuro will be known as the breeding ground for Ottawa soccer players. Training top players takes centre stage as we develop them to play at the highest level possible.

Philosophy

Joga Bonito: possession-based attacking soccer, with the freedom of movement to create confident players who show creativity and flair.

Joga Bonito.
The beautiful game.

We switch the ball across the width of the field to unbalance opponents, then play penetrating balls forward and let our players express themselves in the attacking third.

When we lose the ball, every player gets behind it right away to win it back as quickly as possible and counter-attack with speed and purpose.

  • Attacking principles: width, depth and support, mobility, penetration and improvisation
  • Build from the back: quality passing out of the defending third as the first option
  • Combination play among midfielders in the middle third
  • Individual creativity: 1v1 moves in the attacking third
  • Defending principles: delay, concentration, depth, balance, patience and restraint

The 7 pillars.

We link the Ontario curriculum's six learning skills (responsibility, organization, independent work, collaboration, initiative and self-regulation) to the first four pillars, so effort carries from the pitch to the classroom.

01

Spiritually grounded

Plays fair. Does not lie, cheat or steal.

02

Mentally strong

Stays enthusiastic, confident and positive. Controls emotions and doesn't dwell on mistakes.

03

Socially confident

Shows leadership, deals well with teammates, coaches, parents and media, and takes responsibility for self and team.

04

Educationally sound

Works to keep good grades and hands work in on time. Minimum standards apply, with an Education Advisor interview every reporting period.

05

Physically capable

Speed, endurance, athleticism, quickness, agility, core strength and flexibility.

06

Technical genius

Technical speed: quick, clean receiving, running with the ball, retaining it and releasing it.

07

Tactically intelligent

Recognises and reacts fast. Knows how to attack and defend whether the game is in balance or out of balance.
